Concepts behind the demo

  • Ridge patterns. Prints fall into broad families such as arch, loop and whorl. The library has a whorl (A), a left loop (B), a right loop (C) and an arch (D).
  • Minutiae. Points where a ridge ends or splits in two. Their positions and directions are what the matcher compares.
  • Why not compare images? Two impressions of one finger differ in rotation, position, pressure and noise, so a pixel comparison fails. The minutiae stay the same.
  • Genuine and impostor. A genuine attempt compares two impressions of the same finger; an impostor attempt compares different fingers. The threshold decides the accept or reject outcome.
